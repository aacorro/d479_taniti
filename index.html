Welcome to Taniti

A small tropical island in the Pacific.

Discover the island

Taniti has sandy and rocky beaches, rainforests, a small harbor, and an active volcano. The island is less than 500 square miles and is home to about 20,000 indigenous residents.

Fishing and farming were the main parts of the economy before tourism began to grow.
