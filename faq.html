Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information for your visit.

Currency and payment

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Banks offer currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

Electricity

Power outlets use 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Medical care and safety

There is one hospital and several clinics. Many hospital employees speak more than one language.

Violent crime is very rare, but pickpocketing and other small thefts have increased. Keep your belongings secure.

Language

Many younger residents speak fluent English. Older residents in rural areas may speak very little English.

Alcohol rules

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Holidays and closures

Taniti has many national holidays. Some attractions and restaurants close on these days. Check with the provider before you visit.
