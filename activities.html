Activities

Find things to do on the island.

Beaches

Enjoy the beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay near Taniti City.

Rainforest

Explore the rainforest on a hike or try zip-lining.

Volcano

Visit the small, active volcano in the mountainous center of the island.

Entertainment

Visit the history museum, art galleries, arcade, bowling alley, or movie theater. There are also pubs, a microbrewery, and a dance club.

Tours and outdoor activities

Options include chartered fishing, snorkeling, helicopter rides, and boat or bus tours.

See how to get around

Sightseeing and Merriton Landing

Taniti City has local architecture and nearby white-sand beaches. Many activities are in Merriton Landing, on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay. This area is easy to explore on foot.

A nine-hole golf course is planned for next year. It is not listed as open yet.

Plan around national holiday closures.
