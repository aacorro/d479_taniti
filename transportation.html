Transportation

Learn how to reach Taniti and get around.

Getting to Taniti

Most visitors arrive by air. The small airport accepts small jets and propeller planes. An airport expansion is planned so larger jets can land in the next few years.

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Public and private buses

Public bus hours: 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day.

Public buses serve Taniti City. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis and rental cars

Taxis are available in Taniti City. A local rental car agency is near the airport.

Bikes and walking

Several vendors rent bikes and helmets. Helmets are required by law.

Taniti City is mostly flat and easy to walk around. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.
